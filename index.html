The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

1. At a glance

  • No account registration is required.
  • The App does not contain advertising SDKs or in-app purchases.
  • We collect limited app usage and technical analytics to understand and improve the App.
  • Analytics are processed using Google Firebase Analytics and Konselit Studio's analytics infrastructure, which uses Supabase.
  • Favorites and theme preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following information locally:

  • Favorite or starred cheat codes
  • Theme preference (light, dark, or system)

These preferences are stored using on-device storage, such as AsyncStorage, and are not uploaded as a favorites list or settings profile to our analytics backend.

We use Firebase Analytics and our own analytics infrastructure to measure App usage, diagnose usage patterns, and improve content and navigation. Depending on the platform, configuration, and features used, analytics may include:

  • App interaction events, such as app/session starts, screen views, and cheat-code copy actions

  • App version, operating system, device category or model, and language or locale information

Firebase Analytics may also process device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted by device settings and SDK configuration. Our use of analytics is for App measurement and improvement, not to serve personalized advertising. Technical requests to service providers may involve processing IP addresses for service operation and security.

We do not ask for your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, or microphone recordings. We do not intentionally include your name, email address, or phone number in analytics events.

4. Why we use this information

  • Understand which screens and features are used

We do not sell analytics information. The App does not use the collected events to show personalized ads.

5. Clipboard

When you choose to copy a cheat code, the App writes the selected code to your device's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. A copy-action analytics event may be recorded; however, the App does not read unrelated clipboard content or upload the contents of your clipboard to our analytics backend.
