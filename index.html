This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Cheat Codes (“the App”) handles information. The App is an unofficial offline companion for Grand Theft Auto single-player cheat codes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

1. Summary

  • No account registration
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No developer-operated backend that receives your personal data
  • Favorites and theme preference stay on your device

2. Information we store on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device:

  • Favorite / starred cheat codes
  • Theme preference (light / dark / system)

This information is stored using on-device storage (for example AsyncStorage). It is not sent to a Konselit Studio server.

3. Information we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or other contact details inside the App
  • Precise location
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera content
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics event streams
  • Payment or purchase information (the App has no in-app purchases)

4. Clipboard

When you copy a cheat code, the App writes that code to your device clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The App does not read your clipboard for other purposes and does not upload clipboard content.

5. External links and third parties

The App is designed to work offline with bundled content. If a future update opens a browser link or third-party site, that service’s own privacy policy applies. We do not control third-party websites or apps.

App distribution platforms (Apple App Store / Google Play) may collect information under their own policies when you download or update the App.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Because the App does not use accounts or a remote personal-data backend, there is no cloud profile to delete.
